These charts aggregate data client-side by fetching every document in the "users", "tickets", "deposits", "withdrawals", and "enrollments" collections. That's fine at small scale, but as those collections grow it gets slower and reads more documents than necessary — worth replacing with Firestore aggregation queries or precomputed counters (e.g. updated via a Cloud Function) once you have real volume.
